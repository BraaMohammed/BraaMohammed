## 🔨 what i've built

### [bricks](https://github.com/BraaMohammed/bricks) · [live](https://usebricks.xyz) — open source clay.com alternative
i couldn't pay 300$/m for clay. so i built a free one in 7 days.

same table, same columns. your own keys. no credits, no $300-350/m bill. local-first, MIT,
nothing leaves your machine.

**74 ⭐**, 150 cloners a month.

- every column is programmable. one click and it runs raw js, an ai prompt, a puppeteer script, a
  web search agent, or the email finder
- an autonomous agent per column. give it a question, it writes its own queries, reads the pages,
  and keeps searching until it finds something concrete
- the email finder guesses patterns, then verifies them down free tiers. 2-3k confirmed emails a
  month for $0
- dual agent outreach. one writes, the other role-plays the lead and rejects it. loop until it
  passes
- puppeteer runs on your own ip. 30+ tracker domains blocked so pages stop hanging. no scraping
  subscription
- a free ai gateway that falls through 6 free providers until one answers. point any openai sdk at
  localhost and pay nothing

### [microwave ai](https://github.com/BraaMohammed/microwave-ai) — ai agent that writes and runs its own code
built for a yc-backed founder in 4 days on the vercel sandbox sdk, released 2 weeks earlier. shipped
